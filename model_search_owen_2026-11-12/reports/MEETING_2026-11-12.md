# Team meeting, Thu Nov 12, 12:00: model search

Details: `reports/MODEL_SEARCH.md`. Every number below is printed from its source file in `notebooks/model_search_trace.ipynb` (HTML next to it). Val split only (Oct 19–23); the test split was never read.

## What was searched

The search covered 24 configurations in 68 runs on data-v1 with the weather fix (SHA-1 263bb791…):

- MLP width and depth, and the station embedding size.
- A GRU over the three lag counts.
- Learning rate, batch size, weight decay and dropout (0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5).
- LightGBM as a non-deep reference.

Candidates were ranked at 4 epochs. The finalists were trained fully with seeds 0–2.

## What won, by how much, against what noise

The pick is an **MLP with 2 × 256 hidden units and dropout 0.1** (82,306 parameters, about 9 epochs). Val skill against persistence:

| | Classic, all rows | E-bike, all rows | Classic, low rows | E-bike, low rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chosen, mean ± sd over 3 seeds | 0.109 ± 0.001 | 0.109 ± 0.001 | 0.092 ± 0.002 | 0.080 ± 0.002 |
| Nov 10 base (2 × 64) | 0.103 ± 0.003 | 0.102 ± 0.003 | 0.088 ± 0.0005 | 0.075 ± 0.001 |
| LightGBM (untuned) | 0.106 | 0.099 | 0.094 | 0.080 |
| Best cheap baseline | 0.052 | 0.020 | 0.028 | 0.011 |

- **Against the base:** +0.006 / +0.007. That is 2.3 / 2.6 standard errors once both seed noise and the station-level val sampling are counted. It is positive on 5 of 5 val days for classic and 4 of 5 for e-bikes (Fig. a; `val_noise_gap.csv`).
- **Where the gain comes from:** dropout. Without it, bigger nets overfit after 4–6 epochs and end level with the base (Fig. b). The width helps e-bikes only; on classic bikes, 2 × 64 with dropout is as good.
- **LightGBM:** level with the chosen model on the low rows, the table's hard cases (−0.002 / −0.000, within noise). The deep model's e-bike lead (+0.010) comes from the 7.4 % of e-bike rows with 3 or more e-bikes (Fig. d).
- **Horizon:** the lead is largest at long horizons for e-bikes. At h = 14 min: 0.132 against 0.116 (base) and 0.119 (LightGBM) (Fig. c).
- **Dropout 0.3 scores best** (0.112 / 0.111), but its lead over 0.1 is within noise except on classic all rows (z = 2.2), it is nil on the low rows, and it needs twice the epochs.

## Test result (added Nov 12, 01:45; read once, protocol fixed before in `src/42_test_score.py`)

Test split: Oct 24–28, 1,747,660 rows, starting on a Saturday. Skill against persistence, mean ± sd over seeds 0–2 (`test_team_summary.csv`):

| | Classic, all rows | E-bike, all rows | Classic, low rows | E-bike, low rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chosen 2 × 256, dropout 0.1 | 0.080 ± 0.004 | 0.095 ± 0.002 | 0.070 ± 0.001 | 0.057 ± 0.004 |
| Nov 10 base (2 × 64) | 0.079 ± 0.001 | 0.082 ± 0.005 | 0.066 ± 0.002 | 0.045 ± 0.003 |
| LightGBM | 0.071 ± 0.002 | 0.084 ± 0.001 | 0.070 ± 0.002 | 0.060 ± 0.001 |
| Best cheap baseline | 0.011 | 0.018 | 0.007 | 0.007 |

- **E-bikes:** the gain holds on test: +0.013 over the base and +0.011 over LightGBM.
- **Classic:** the val gain over the base (+0.006) does not carry over to test (+0.001). The reviews had flagged it as weak.
- **Lower skill than on val:** the test week starts with a weekend, and val has none. Chosen model, classic: 0.061 and 0.034 on Sat and Sun, against 0.114–0.117 on Mon and Tue (`test_team_by_day.csv`).
- **Unseen stations:** 2,324 test rows (0.13 %) are at 2 stations not seen in training. Without them, every skill changes by 0.0002 or less (`test_team_unseen.csv`).
- Nothing was tuned after the test score.

## What the reviews changed

Six independent reviews (Opus and Fable, three effort levels each) found no leakage and confirmed the choice. Fixed after them:

- A wrong causal claim about dropout.
- An overstated noise ratio: now 2.3–2.6 standard errors, not "2–5 ×".
- Five number slips, for example 20 inputs, not 21.
- The LightGBM tie on the low rows, which is now stated.

They also asked for checks, which now ran:

- Dropout on the GRU and on 3 × 256: no gain.
- Dropout 0.3 and 0.5: skill peaks at 0.2–0.3.
- A per-day and per-station bootstrap.

## Decisions for the team (recommendation first)

1. **Model for the progress report (due Fri Nov 13, 11 pm):** adopt 2 × 256 with dropout 0.1, with the base and LightGBM as references. Alternative: keep the Nov 10 base. The gain is small (+0.006 / +0.007) but consistent, and fully traced.
2. **Dropout rate for the final report:** keep 0.1 now, and settle 0.1 against 0.3 on data not used for selection. One way is to refit on train to Oct 16 and check on Oct 17–18, which is also a weekend check, since val has no weekend. Alternative: switch to 0.3 now.
3. **Test protocol: done** (Nov 12; three seeds, mean ± sd, read once). Any later model gets its own single test score, and the dropout choice can no longer use the test split.
4. **LightGBM in the report:** show it as the non-deep reference beside the cheap baselines. It shows where the deep model helps (e-bike rows with 3 or more e-bikes) and where it does not (low rows).
